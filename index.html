The math behind that text

Every job gets the same simple math. Nothing is hidden.

Profit = job price + billed extra work − materials − crew hours × your hourly crew cost

You set your hourly crew cost once, with pay and taxes. If a job has no price yet, or nobody sent hours, we say so. We never show a profit that leaves out labor.

What it catches

Receipt apps file your paper. Bottom Dollar watches the money on each job and tells you when something looks off.

Work you may not have billed

Extra work you texted in
Text "Smith: moved outlet $150" and it shows up every week until you text "Billed Smith."
Parts bought after the job was done
If receipts keep coming in for a finished job, we ask if you billed for them.
Receipts with no job
Receipts nobody tied to a job get two reminders, then land on your weekly list.

Jobs that are going bad

The 80% heads-up
When a job's costs pass 80% of its price, you get a text while there is still time to act.
Jobs that are losing money
If costs go over the price, the weekly text says so and asks if there was extra work you have not billed.
Missing hours or prices
We remind you, at most twice a week per job. The text says "No hours sent" instead of a fake profit.

Questions

Do I need to download an app?

No. You text a phone number. The only website you ever need is for signup, adding crew phones, and the bookkeeper page.

What if I forget to say which job?

We text back and ask. The text lists your open jobs so you can answer with one word.

Why do you ask about crew hours?

Receipts only show materials. Without hours, a job would look like it made more than it really did. Each Friday we ask, and a quick reply like "Smith 12" is enough.

Does it cost more to add my crew?

No. Every crew phone is included in the one price. Each person agrees to get texts on their own phone.

Do I have to use QuickBooks?

No. The weekly text works without it. If you use QuickBooks Online, you can connect it and receipts go straight in.

Is there a free trial?

No. Instead, you get the 60-day promise. If 60 days of receipts turn up no losing job and no missed charge, you get your money back.

Does it do estimates or invoices?

No. Bottom Dollar does one job: it tells you what each job made and what you may have missed. Keep using what you use now for estimates and invoices.
